# Refunds And Duplicate Payments

Source ID: refunds

Refund requests must be checked against the original payment record before an
agent promises a resolution. For duplicate payments, support should ask for the
invoice number, payment reference, and the last four digits of the payment
method. If the duplicate charge is visible in the payment record, support may
draft a refund ticket update and mark the case for finance review.

Refunds for cancelled workshops are eligible only when the cancellation was
made at least seven days before the workshop date. Exceptional refunds require
human review and must not be promised by the assistant.
