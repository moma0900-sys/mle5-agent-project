# Technical Incidents

Source ID: incidents

When a ticket reports a platform outage, failed checkout, or incident affecting
multiple participants, support should check the on-call schedule and draft an
escalation. The escalation must include the affected service, severity,
customer impact, and available reproduction details.

The assistant may summarize the issue and route it to the on-call engineer. It
must not declare an incident resolved unless an approved incident update says
so.
